# Tagaholic

Tagaholic helps an individual barbershop singer find, learn and keep track of tags from barbershoptags.com.

## Language

**Singer**:
The person using Tagaholic: an individual barbershop singer finding and learning tags.
_Avoid_: User, member

**Tag**:
A short barbershop arrangement, usually the ending of a song, as published on barbershoptags.com and identified by its barbershoptags.com id. Different versions of the same title are different Tags.
_Avoid_: Song, arrangement, version

**Part**:
One voice in a Tag. A Tag has as many Parts as its part count: Tenor, Lead, Baritone and Bass, then a 5th Part, 6th Part and so on. A 3-part Tag has a 1st, 2nd and 3rd Part instead.
_Avoid_: Voice, Bari, line, Tenor 2

**Key**:
The tonality and tonic a Tag is written in, such as G-flat major.
_Avoid_: WritKey

**Pitch**:
The tonic note of a Tag's Key, sounded for as long as the Singer holds the Pitch button, as a pitch pipe sounds for as long as it is blown, to set the Key before singing. When the Singer transposes it, the Pitch is the tonic of the key they will sing in.
_Avoid_: Starting note, key note

**Transposition**:
How many semitones the Singer has moved the Pitch up or down from the Tag's Key, up to 11 either way, to sing the Tag in another key. Stepping past 11 rolls it over to 0, an octave from the Key. It starts at 0 each time the Tag is opened. A transposed Pitch is tuned in just intonation: a pure interval from the Key's tonic, which stays at standard pitch.
_Avoid_: Offset, shift, capo

## Keeping track

**Sung Part**:
A Part the Singer has sung on a particular Tag at least once. It does not claim the Singer still knows it.
_Avoid_: Known part, learned part

**Folder**:
A named group of Tags: either one the Singer made, or the built-in Bookmarks. A Tag can be in several Folders, and Folders are independent of Sung Parts.
_Avoid_: List, playlist, collection, setlist

**Bookmarks**:
The built-in Folder that the Bookmark button adds a Tag to. A Tag is **Bookmarked** when it is in this Folder.
_Avoid_: Favourites, wishlist, starred

**Repertoire**:
Every Tag on which the Singer has at least one Sung Part. A Tag enters and leaves the Repertoire only through its Sung Parts.
_Avoid_: Library, my tags, collection

## Media

**Learning Track**:
An audio recording of a Tag in which one Part is emphasised, for learning that Part.
_Avoid_: Part track, predominant, teach track

**Full Mix**:
An audio recording of a Tag with all Parts balanced.
_Avoid_: AllParts, all-parts track

**Teaching Video**:
A video of someone teaching a Tag.
_Avoid_: TeachVid, tutorial
